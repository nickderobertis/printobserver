## What to answer with

Answer with the assessment document alone: one line saying what is happening,
how sure you are, whether the print should carry on, what you did, why you did
it, and whether you are escalating to a person. Say plainly when you are not
sure — a low-confidence reading a person can act on is worth more than a
confident guess.
